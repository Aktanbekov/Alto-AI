How to read all of this

  • Reviews are posted voluntarily, and approvals are shared more eagerly than refusals, so the approval share here sits well above any real base rate.
  • Consulate differences partly reflect *who applies where* (program mix, funding profiles, applicant pools), not just officer behaviour.
  • The useful signal is what gets asked and how well-prepared answers look, not a predicted probability of approval. Use the question mix to decide what to rehearse.
